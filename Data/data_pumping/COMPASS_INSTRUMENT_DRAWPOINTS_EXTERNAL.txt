=====================
 TEXTUAL DESCRIPTION 
=====================

ABSTRACT: 
Drawpoint related to a licence to take water from a watercourse, well and/or underground source in a proclaimed groundwater or surface water area, or as otherwise prescribed by regulations (section 5C). Instrument drawpoints may also be related to licences to construct or alter a well, including bores and soaks (section 26D); or to interfere with the waters, bed and banks of a watercourse on crown land; or any watercourse, race, drain, dam, reservoir or disused watercourse, race or drain on freehold land, or land granted or demised by the Crown (sections 11, 17 and 21A).

The instrument drawpoint is the published location for a water abstraction point or location of interference to the bed and banks of a watercourse.


ANZLIC SEARCH WORDS: 
Water, Waterbore, Well, Bore, Licence, Soak, Crossing, Bed and Banks

==============	
 DATA QUALITY
==============

LINEAGE:
Instrument drawpoints show the location of water source. Data sourced from the Departments COMPASS system. The Instrument drawpoint represents the discrete location of water source related to an inforce instrument. Instrument Drawpoints also show location of expired drawpoints.

Updates:
Compass was commissioned in June 2018 and is subject to regularly enhancements and bug fixes.

POSITIONAL ACCURACY:
Instrument drawpoints are provided as GDA2020 coordinates and represent a discrete location for location of water source, the instrument drawpoint represents the location of water source for an inforce instrument.

ATTRIBUTE ACCURACY:
Accurate, instrument drawpoint location is validated by the custodian and represent actual location of water source.

LOGICAL CONSISTENCY:
The instrument drawpoint represents the discrete location of a water source related to a licence/instrument.

COMPLETENESS:
Complete.
